### 🏥 About NovaDent
**NovaDent Dental Clinic & Health Tourism Center** is a premier dental healthcare institution located in Maslak, Istanbul's premier business and financial district. We combine cutting-edge dental technology with patient-centric boutique healthcare.

Fully accredited by the Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Health for International Health Tourism, our clinic welcomes thousands of domestic and international patients every year for multidisciplinary dental care ranging from digital smile design to surgical implantology and clear aligner orthodontics.
